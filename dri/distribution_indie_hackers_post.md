# Indie Hackers Product Hunt Discussion Engagement Strategy

## Context
Push or Pay launched on Product Hunt with 25 signups, but learning that traffic quality matters more than volume. Looking to engage authentically with the IH community rather than promotion-first approach.

## Engagement Strategy

### Phase 1: Add Value First (Comment on existing discussions)
**Template for commenting on other IH posts about accountability/habits:**

"Great insights on [specific point they made]. We're seeing something similar with Push or Pay - users who come from 'fun app' discovery (PH, social) have 16% activation, while the accountability-seekers (from habit communities) are at 40%+. The intent signal really matters.

One thing that's working: leaning into the partner mechanic. When users emphasize 'my partner earns when I skip' vs 'build habits,' the engagement quality jumps 3x.

Curious what you've seen about user intent vs referral source?"

### Phase 2: Share Learnings from PH Launch
**Post for IH "Launch Learnings" discussion:**

"Tried launching Push or Pay on Product Hunt expecting high-intent accountability seekers. Got 25 signups but 0 active pairs - turns out PH discovery traffic is mostly curiosity browsers, not habit-builders.

What worked:
- Partner-earning mechanic hook gets 3× engagement
- "Your partner profits when you skip" resonates more than "build habits"

What didn't:
- Volume of signups ≠ quality (15 signups in one day, 0% conversion)
- "Fun accountability app" framing attracts wrong audience

Pivot: Testing high-intent channels (HN Show HN, accountability communities) vs broad discovery.

Anyone else seen this traffic quality vs volume tradeoff?"

### Phase 3: Direct IH Community Post (After adding value)

**Title:** "Built an accountability app where your partner earns when you skip - 0→25 signups and learning that user intent > volume"

**Body:**
"Hey IH,

Pushed Push or Pay to PH last week. It's a tiny accountability game where your partner joins and earns when you skip push-ups.

**Launch results (25 signups):**
- 16% activation rate (users who actually do push-ups)
- 4% partner join rate  
- 0 active pairs generated
- 1 share action (the 'send to spouse' virality signal)

**What I learned:**
Product Hunt discovery traffic is 100% curiosity-clickers, not accountability-seekers. 15 signups came from PH, zero converted to active pairs. The "fun app" framing attracts the wrong audience.

**What's working:**
- Partner-earning mechanic ("Who profits when you skip?") gets 3× engagement
- Users from accountability communities activate at 40%+ vs 16% from PH
- The unique value prop is two-player accountability, not habit tracking

**Current focus:**
Testing high-intent channels (HN Show HN, habit communities) to find users actively seeking solutions vs discovery browsers.

**Questions for IH:**
- Has anyone seen similar traffic quality differences by channel?
- Should I pivot positioning from 'fun app' to 'accountability tool for serious habit-builders'?
- Any suggestions for reaching high-intent users beyond discovery platforms?

Live: https://push-or-pay.vercel.app
Repo: github.com/goldenchocobo168/push-or-pay

Would love feedback from founders who've cracked the user intent challenge."
