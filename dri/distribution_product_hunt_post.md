# Push or Pay - Product Hunt Submission

## Product Name
Push or Pay

## Tagline
The accountability app where your partner gets paid when you skip your push-ups

## One-liner description
A fun accountability game between partners: you protect your streak, they get a little too excited when you fail. The streak is the hero; the penalty is the joke.

## Full description
Push or Pay is NOT a fitness app. It's a tiny game between two people that helps you build tiny habits through accountability.

**How it works:**
1. Set a daily push-up target (start small — 5-10 is perfect)
2. Choose a penalty amount ($5, $10, or go wild with $100)
3. Invite your partner (they'll get paid when you miss days)
4. Log your push-ups daily or pay the penalty

**Why it actually works:**
- **Social accountability** — your partner is the "final boss" who benefits when you slack off
- **Tiny targets** — starts with just 5-10 push-ups, achievable even on lazy days
- **Fun over guilt** — the penalty is a joke between you two, not a guilt trip
- **Streak protection** — you're defending something, not just "being good"

**Perfect for:**
- Couples who want to build habits together
- Friends who want to gamify fitness
- Anyone who's failed at solo fitness apps

The streak is the hero. The penalty is the joke. Your relationship is the product.

**Tech stack:** Node.js, Vercel, Upstash Redis, vanilla JS
