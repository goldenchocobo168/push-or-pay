# Discord/Slack Community Engagement Strategy

## Philosophy
Authentic participation first, tool mention second. Add value to accountability discussions before sharing Push or Pay.

## Target Communities
- r/habitbuilding Discord (if accessible)
- Indie Hackers Discord
- Habit-building Slack groups
- Productivity/accountability communities

## Engagement Protocol

### Step 1: Listen and Add Value (No Tool Mentions)
**Time investment:** 1-2 hours of reading existing discussions

**Value-add comments:**
- Share accountability frameworks that worked
- Discuss behavioral science insights
- Help others troubleshoot their habit challenges
- Celebrate others' wins

### Step 2: Contextual Tool Mentions (After Establishing Credibility)
**Template for when accountability apps come up:**

"I've tried a bunch of trackers too - what finally worked for me was making it social but not shameful. Built a tiny app where my partner joins and 'protects' her streak - when I skip, she earns the penalty.

It's funny because now I'm not just letting myself down, I'm making someone else richer (even if just virtual bubble tea money).

Different from most trackers because the partner is the main character, not the dashboards.

If that two-player angle sounds useful, happy to share more. Otherwise, what's worked for your accountability setup?"

### Step 3: Respond to Interest with Value, Not Pitch
**When someone asks for more:**

"Sure! The core insight was that guilt-based accountability fails, but playful competition works. In Push or Pay:

- Your partner joins as 'streak protector'
- They see your progress live  
- When you skip, THEY earn (not you)
- Creates friendly rivalry vs shame

Early data: users who emphasize the partner mechanic activate 3× more than 'habit tracker' framing.

Key learning: user intent matters more than volume. 25 PH signups → 0% pairs, but accountability community signups → 40% activation.

Happy to dig into any specific part - the mechanics, the tech stack, or what didn't work."

## Topics to Avoid
- Direct promotion without context
- Spamming multiple channels with identical content
- Comparing negatively to other tools
- Over-promising results

## Success Metrics
- Authentic engagement (replies, discussions)
- Quality signups (40%+ activation target)
- Community trust (no negative feedback)
- Long-term relationships (follow-up discussions)

## Fallback Plan
If communities don't respond well to tool mentions, pivot to pure value-add: share accountability insights, behavioral science, and learnings without mentioning the app. Build relationships first.
