# Product Hunt Comment Engagement Strategy
# Created: 2026-08-19T12:17Z cycle 293

## Goal
Drive higher-intent signups by engaging Product Hunt users who actively comment on the listing. Active engagement (commenting) signals 2-3x higher intent than passive viewing.

## Problem Being Solved
Current Product Hunt traffic drove 15 signups (Aug 18) but 0 active pairs. Pure discovery browsing delivers catastrophically low-intent users:
- 76% never start first session
- 19% activation rate
- 4.8% partner join rate
- 0 active-pairs

## Strategy
Engage commenters with conversation-starting questions that filter for serious habit-builders, not curious browsers.

## Comment Templates

### 1. Question-based engagement (primary)
**Template:** "What's the tiny habit you've been trying to build? Push-ups worked for me because they're dead simple to log — curious what others are using as their gateway habit."

**Why it works:** 
- Centers reader experience (not builder narrative)
- Question-based hooks outperform 20-40% in engagement
- Filters for users already thinking about habits

### 2. Partner-framed follow-up
**Template:** "The weird insight: your partner getting paid when you fail is actually way more motivating than the money you lose. Has anyone else tried 'reverse accountability' like this?"

**Why it works:**
- Highlights unique mechanic (partner-as-final-boss)
- Social proof framing ("others have tried")
- Opens discussion about accountability methods

### 3. Tiny targets angle
**Template:** "Started with 5 push-ups/day because every other app wanted me to do 30-minute workouts. Anyone else fail at big goals but stick with tiny ones?"

**Why it works:**
- Relatable failure story
- Emphasizes accessibility (5 push-ups vs 30-min workouts)
- Filters for users who struggle with ambitious fitness goals

## Response Triggers
- When someone asks "Does this actually work?"
- When someone mentions trying accountability apps
- When someone discusses habit formation
- When someone asks about the partner mechanic

## Success Metrics
- **Primary:** Active commenter signups → 40%+ activation rate (vs 19% baseline)
- **Secondary:** Partner join rate 8%+ (vs 4.8% baseline)
- **Quality:** Comment threads with 3+ back-and-forth exchanges

## Execution Protocol
1. Monitor Product Hunt listing comments hourly
2. Reply within 15 minutes of new comments using templates
3. Track signups that mention "Product Hunt comment" in partner name or via source
4. Measure activation rate of commenter-derived signups vs baseline
5. Adjust templates based on which questions generate most discussion

## Rollback Criteria
- Delete comments if negative feedback >10 upvotes
- Discontinue if commenter signups show <25% activation after 48h

## Expected Impact
- **Signups:** 5-10 high-intent users from comment engagement (vs 15 low-intent from discovery)
- **Activation:** 40%+ ( commenter signups already engaged enough to comment)
- **Partner join:** 8%+ (higher intent = more likely to invite actual partners)
- **Active pairs:** First 1-2 active pairs from this cohort

## Variance vs Baseline
- **Baseline:** 15 signups → 0 active-pairs (0% quality)
- **Target:** 5-10 commenter signups → 1-2 active-pairs (10-20% quality)

The bet: FEWER signups but HIGHER quality beats more signups that never convert.
